# Photos

Drop Brandon's real job photos here. Nothing else.

- **No stock photos.** Not of trees, not of crews, not of bucket trucks. A stock
  photo of somebody else's crew on a tree service site is the fastest way to look
  like the twelve other sites the homeowner already closed.
- Prefer real GBP photos: the fallen tree at the church/daycare, removals in
  progress, the stump grinder, Brandon on the job.
- Name them for what they show: `pine-removal-near-wires.jpg`, not `IMG_4471.jpg`.
- Every photo needs alt text describing the work AND the place — that is written
  at the point of use, not here.

Until a photo lands, the hero and service pages render the `.end-grain` textured
panel. That is deliberate, not an oversight.
